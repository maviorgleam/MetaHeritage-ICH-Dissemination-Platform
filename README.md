# MetaHeritage-ICH-Dissemination-Platform
An immersive ancient-style ICH market with multimodal AI digital human interaction.

## About This Project
This is a design research project exploring immersive dissemination models for intangible cultural heritage (ICH) in metaverse scenarios, as well as sustainable operational pathways integrating culture and commerce.

This repository contains the core interaction modules and research analysis scripts I personally designed and developed during the team project, to demonstrate my work in scene design, interaction system development and empirical evaluation for cultural heritage metaverse scenarios.

## Research Background
Traditional ICH dissemination faces three practical challenges:
- Offline markets are limited by time, geography and weather, with narrow audience reach
- Online static text-image displays lack interactivity and immersion, leading to low knowledge retention
- ICH interpretation relies heavily on offline inheritors, unable to support large-scale, round-the-clock public outreach

This project investigates whether immersive metaverse scenes combined with multimodal AI digital humans can effectively improve ICH dissemination efficiency.

## Core Design & My Contributions
**Role**: Core Design Researcher

I led the overall scene design and spatial environment development of the ICH metaverse ancient-style market, designed the AI digital human interaction system, conducted research on immersive communication models, and oversaw full-process user validation.

**My Contributions**：
### 1. Immersive Marketplace Scene Design & Spatial Development
Designed and delivered a comprehensive immersive traditional-style marketplace experience.
- Planned the overall spatial layout and touring route based on cultural narrative rhythm
- Designed interactive trigger zones for each stall to match the embodied interaction experience
- Built the step-by-step craft disassembly mechanism, turning static exhibits into dynamic interactive demonstrations

Full spatial design details and iteration process are compiled in the supporting design documentation.

### 2. Multimodal AI Digital Human Dialogue System
Designed and participated in the development of a multimodal AI digital human dialogue system.
Established the dual-mode interaction logic of active introduction and free Q&A, to enable round-the-clock interactive interpretation of intangible cultural heritage knowledge.

### 3. Quantitative Validation of Dissemination Effect
Conducted three controlled experimental studies to quantitatively verify the propagation gain of metaverse dissemination patterns.
Adopted a mixed-methods evaluation framework across three dimensions: exploration interest, knowledge retention, and cultural identity.
